Welcome to AnimeOpen

Your Complete Quick-Start & Feature Guide
01 📁

Load Any Anime Folder

AnimeOpen scans your local drives for video files (.mkv, .mp4) and even streams anime stored directly inside .zip archives without unzipping.

02 ✨

Automated AniList HD Art

Automatically retrieves HD covers, banner backdrops, voice actor showcases, airing countdowns, and theme songs. Artwork is cached locally on disk so it works offline forever.

03 🎬

Cinematic Streaming & AniSkip

True HTTP 206 instant seeking across multi-gigabyte files, automated opening/ending skipping, audio/subtitle track selector, and 1-click external player launch (VLC/MPV).

04 🌐

In-App Browser & Torrents

Direct download & built-in WebTorrent engine. Downloads automatically create and save episodes into organized series subfolders (e.g. D:\Anime\Frieren\).

05 🎨

6 Themes & Multitasking

Switch between Midnight OLED, Cyberpunk Neo-Tokyo, Sakura Bloom, and more. Multitask freely between the browser and library with zero page reloads.

06 ⌨️

Essential Shortcuts

Space Play/Pause • ←/→ Seek 10s • ↑/↓ Volume • S Screenshot Frame • F Fullscreen • M Mute
